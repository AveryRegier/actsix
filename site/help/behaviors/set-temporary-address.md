## Setting a Temporary Address

If a member is temporarily staying somewhere other than their home (such as a hospital, rehab facility, or nursing home), you can record a temporary address.

### Adding a Temporary Address

1. Open the **Edit Member** form for the member.
2. Scroll to the **Temporary Location** section.
3. Start typing the location name in the **Location** search field. A dropdown list of known locations appears.
   - Select a location from the list, or type a custom location name.

![Temporary address location search](../images/edit-member-temp-address.png)

4. Fill in:
   - **Room Number** — the specific room or unit number (optional)
   - **Start Date** — when the temporary stay began
   - **Notes** — any additional details (e.g., visiting hours)

5. Click **Save** to save the temporary address.

### Removing a Temporary Address

1. Open the **Edit Member** form.
2. In the **Temporary Location** section, clear the location field or use the remove option.
3. Click **Save**.

> The current temporary location appears on the Household page and in the Record Contact form, so deacons know where to reach the member.
