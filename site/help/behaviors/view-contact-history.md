## Viewing Contact History

The contact history for a household shows all recorded contacts for that household's members.

1. Open the **Household** page.
2. Scroll down to the **Contact History** section.

![Contact history section on household page](../images/household-contact-history.png)

The contact history table shows:

- **Date** — when the contact occurred
- **Type** — how contact was made (phone, visit, text, etc.)
- **Who contacted** — which deacon or participant made the contact
- **Members** — which household members were involved
- **Summary** — notes recorded about the interaction
- **Follow-up** — whether a follow-up is required

### Recording a New Contact

To add a new contact entry, click the **Record Contact** button in the Contact History section.

After you submit a new contact, it appears in Contact History.
