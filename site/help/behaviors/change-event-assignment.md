## Changing Event Assignments

Use this when a position is open or when you need to move someone to a different role.

1. Open **Event Assignments** from a Sign Ups date link.
2. On the event row, click the name in **Assigned To** (or click **Open**).
3. In the assignment panel, type a name in the search box.
4. Select one person, then click **Save**.
   - The page confirms with **Assignments updated.**
   - The selected person is assigned to that position.
5. To clear a position instead, open the same panel and click **Clear Assignment**.

Tip: If you do not see clickable assignment controls, your account does not currently have assignment-edit access for that event.
