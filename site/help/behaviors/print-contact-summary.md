## Printing the Contact Summary Report

The Contact Summary Report is formatted for printing with a printer-friendly layout.

1. Go to the **Contact Summary** page and apply any filters you want (the printed report reflects the current filter).
2. Use your browser's print function:
   - **Windows / Linux:** press `Ctrl + P`
   - **Mac:** press `Cmd + P`
   - Or use your browser menu → **File → Print**
3. In the print dialog, select your printer and click **Print**.

The printed report uses a condensed layout with additional columns (including a Summary column) that are hidden on screen to save space. Headers and rows are formatted to fit on standard paper.

> **Tip:** Use **Print Preview** in your browser to check how many pages the report will use before printing.
