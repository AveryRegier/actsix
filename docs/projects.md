# ActSix Projects feature

We have a need to track projects that the deacons manage for those in need at the church.

## Project phases

Projects have several phases.
1. Discovery
2. Vetting
3. Funding
4. Preparation
5. Implementation

### Discovery

There are several ways that projects can be found.  
* Announcements in front of the congregation lead to people going to the church app, go to the deacons page, and fill in a form telling us about either their need or that they have something to donate.
* Sometimes we ask the congregation to nominate needs they know about from neighbors or people they are ministering to.
* They may come from people we know in the community who are in need such as single mothers, families of deployed service members, shut ins, elderly, etc.
* We have a list of widows in need, shut ins, cancer patients, families in deep financial need, etc who we call on regularly and can learn of needs they have.

Typically those needs will come to be known by someone in leadership of the deacons.
Sometimes we have had a kiosk that deacons man at which projects and needs can be heard about from members.

### Vetting
We want to ensure we are helping the right people in the right way for the right reasons.

In Acts 6, the deacons were established to meet the needs of believing widows as they had often no family to help them. With that example in mind, we want to verify the following:
1) Is the person we are helping a member of the church?  This generally needs to be the case unless we are specifically looking to reach out to the community.
2) Does the person have family that can help to meet their needs?
3) Motivation: is the person truly in need, or trying to get something for free.

We can talk with the church office to find out some background on the person to answer these questions.  Planning Center also has membership information in it.

The project itself also must be vetted.  
* Do the deacons have the capability to complete it?
* Should this be done by a professional?
* Are there other ministries or organizations better suited to help the person we can refer them to?
* Is it safe for us to work on the project?
* Urgency: how much time does the person have to solve the problem?


### Funding

Sometimes the member can pay for the work, they just don't have the capability (strength, ability, know how, or knowledge) to do the work.

Sometimes we will work with the Benevolence team of the deacon ministry to assess their capabillity to pay and the available budget of the benevolence ministry.

Sometimes the cost is minimal or within the means of a member of the congregation who wants to bless others in the congregation.

The cost of the project should be estimated during this phase.

### Preparation

We need to put together a plan for doing the work.
* Materials
* Tools
* Worksers
* Time/Scheduling

Sometimes we will want to redirect the project to a small group, or youth team, or missions team looking to raise money.

### Decision gate
 Who may approve proceeding?  Is there some kind of limit on project size and who should be involved in approving it?
 
 * Deacon on site decision
 * Lead deacon
 * Deacons
 * Staff
 * Head pastor
 * Elders

### Implementation

Concers during implementation:
* Communication betwen workers, leaders, and the member.
* Status reports as various parts of the project are worked on.
* What blockers or problems have arrison?
* Is the project complete?

## Reporting

There should be a report for montly meetings:
* Who is being helped
* How to contact them.
* Latest status of the project
* Project phase
* People involved in meeting the need
* Cost? (This could be considered sensitive information)
